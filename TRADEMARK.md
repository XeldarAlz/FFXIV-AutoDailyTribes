# Auto Daily Tribes Trademark and Naming Policy

**Last updated:** 13 September 2026

Auto Daily Tribes is free software under the AGPL-3.0-or-later ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)), and you are free to fork it, change it, and distribute your version. That license covers the code. It does not cover the name.

"Auto Daily Tribes", the plugin's internal name `AutoDailyTribes`, the plugin icon, and the visual identity built from them are the project's marks. They tell a player which build they are installing and who stands behind it, and they are not licensed with the source.

## If you fork Auto Daily Tribes

Give your version its own name, its own internal name, and its own icon before you publish it.

- Do not call it Auto Daily Tribes, and do not use a name that would be mistaken for it, including "Auto Daily Tribes" with a word attached on either side
- Do not reuse the internal name `AutoDailyTribes`. Dalamud keys a plugin's settings and its install record on that name, so a fork that keeps it reads and overwrites the original's settings and cannot be installed next to it
- Do not ship the plugin icon as your own identity
- Do not publish it in a Dalamud plugin repository in a way that suggests it is my build, an official version, or a continuation of this project
- Say plainly what changed, so nobody reports your bugs here
- Credit the original author, as the next section describes

## Credit the original author

Every fork, and every project that reuses this code in whole or in part, keeps a visible credit to where it came from:

```text
Based on Auto Daily Tribes by XeldarAlz
https://github.com/XeldarAlz/FFXIV-AutoDailyTribes
```

Put it in your README or project page, and on your About or credits screen if your plugin has one. This is not a courtesy request: [NOTICE](NOTICE) sets it as an additional term under section 7(b) of the AGPL, so dropping it breaks the license the same way stripping the copyright notice would.

Crediting is not the same as claiming. Under section 7(c), your fork must not present itself as the original, or as written, maintained or endorsed by the original author.

## What you can do without asking

You can name this project. Saying yours is "based on Auto Daily Tribes" or "a fork of Auto Daily Tribes" and linking to this repository are fine anywhere, on top of the credit and the license notices you are required to keep. Writing about Auto Daily Tribes, reviewing it, streaming it, or discussing it needs no permission at all.

The allied tribe emblems on the tribe cards are Square Enix's artwork from FINAL FANTASY XIV. They are not this project's to license, so neither the AGPL nor this page grants you anything in them.

## What needs permission

Using the name or the marks for your own build, repository, community, or anything sold, or in any way that implies this project endorses or runs what you are offering.

## Why

Players install a plugin by name from a repository they trust, and a Dalamud plugin runs inside the game client with the same access the game has. A fork wearing this one's name inherits that trust, and its bug reports land here, for code I did not write and cannot fix. Renaming costs you an hour and settles all of it. The credit costs you one line and keeps the origin of the work visible to everyone who finds yours.
